# Limiting-density route limitations

- The PRISM paper does not say whether its random matrix was drawn from a
  finite beta-Laguerre ensemble, from the limiting density, or by another
  implementation.
- Deterministic midpoint quantiles remove unknown seed variation and test the
  named distribution itself; they do not reconstruct an unpublished sample.
- Figure D.2 has no raw data, so first-hit targets use one-marker brackets.
- This route tests iteration-domain behavior. It does not reproduce A100
  wall-clock timing, which is outside the authorized CPU-only compute.
